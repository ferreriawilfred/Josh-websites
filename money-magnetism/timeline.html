24 Hours

The body changes first

A specific loosening in the chest. The math that runs every night goes quiet — not solved, just quieter. Women describe sleeping differently. Some describe crying without knowing why, then waking up lighter.

The channel is opening. The release is physical before it is financial.

Days 2–4

Small things arrive

From directions you weren’t watching. A Venmo from someone paying back money you forgot they owed. A refund that processes early. A message about a shift, a contract, an opportunity.

Here is what I tell every woman at this stage: stay open. Don’t explain it. Don’t dismiss it. Just let it land.

Day 7+

It compounds

The small arrivals become a pattern. An unexpected check. A bill that settles for less than owed. A raise that wasn’t supposed to happen until next quarter. An old account with money you forgot existed.

This is the week the pattern breaks.
