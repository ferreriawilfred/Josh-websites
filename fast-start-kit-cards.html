This sheet shows you exactly where your money is going and what your average job is really worth — including what you'd make if you closed 30% of your estimates instead of 10%.

What most painters find when they see it: they've been leaving $5,000 to $8,000 a month on the table — not because of bad leads, but because of what happened after the lead came in.

Print it. Put it somewhere you'll see it. Let it remind you what's actually at stake every time you consider skipping a follow-up.
