Lead platforms.

You pay 50-100+ per lead. The same lead went to four other guys. You call first, they already talked to someone cheaper. You do it again next week because you don't have anything else.

✕

Organic posting.

You put up job photos, a before-and-after, "call for a free estimate." Two likes — one from your wife and one from your mother. Maybe the third is from you. Yet nobody calls. So you try again next week.

✕

Word of mouth.

Great when it's working. Completely out of your control when it slows down. No way to turn it back on at will.

✕

Chasing no-shows.

A homeowner books an estimate, then doesn't answer the door. You drove 20 minutes. You text once. No reply. You move on and write off the lead.

✕

Losing at the table.

You give a good estimate, the work is solid, the homeowner seems interested — and then they say "I need to think about it." You smile, say no problem, drive home, and never hear from them again.
