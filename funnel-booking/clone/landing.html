Frequently Asked

Am I going to get a high-pressure sales pitch?

Absolutely not. We’ll give you an honest assessment of what the project involves and what it will cost, and answer any questions you have. No obligations.

How long does the estimate take?

About 45 minutes for smaller projects like kitchen cabinets, and up to an hour and a half for a full interior or exterior. We walk the property with you (15–20 minutes), measure and evaluate conditions, then go through a written estimate line by line (15–20 minutes). We do it in person so changes can be made on the spot.

Do I need to be home during the estimate?

Yes, we ask that all decision makers are present. We bring samples and ask questions most contractors don’t, so the proposal is as accurate as possible.

Can you help me choose colors?

Yes, we’re happy to do a free color consultation. Just ask.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully licensed and insured. We carry liability insurance and workers’ comp, so you’re protected from start to finish.
