# Ad copy — Facebook / Instagram (drafts)

**Audience:** residential painting contractors (US). **Objective:** Leads / Traffic → the Survey page (page 1).
**Landing URL:** your survey page, with UTMs, e.g. `?utm_source=fb&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=booked-estimates&utm_content=A`

No income guarantees, no made-up testimonials. Every number below is already used on your own pages (the $4M / $1M / 25% figures are yours). Keep proof screenshots real.

## Creative (use your own real images only)
- **Image 1:** Lanty photo (Funnel Image1) with short overlay text: "Painters: 7 quick questions."
- **Image 2:** the phone with Stripe + DripJobs notifications, **only if those are real screenshots**.
- **Video (best):** 20–30 second phone video from a job site, unedited: "If you're a painter and leads go cold before you call back, watch this."
- Square 1:1 and vertical 9:16 for Stories/Reels.

## Targeting (starting point)
- US, ages 28–60, Facebook + Instagram, advantage+ placements.
- Interests/behaviors: Small business owners, home improvement, painting contractors, general contractors.
- Exclude: existing leads/customers list.
- Start with 3 ads (A/B/C) in one ad set, then keep the winner.
